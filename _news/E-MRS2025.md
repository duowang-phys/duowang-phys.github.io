From September 15, 2025 to September 18, 2025, two group members attended the 2025 European Materials Research Society (E-MRS) Fall Meeting held at Warsaw University of Technology, Warsaw, Poland, and presented both invited talks and posters. Duo Wang gave an invited talk on the polar magnetic skyrmions discovered in the CrIn*X*$_3$ (X=Se, Te) system. PhD student Fengyi Zhou presented a poster, titled *Revealing rich magnetic phases in orthorhombic perovskite TbCrO3: a first principles study.*

We also had a chance to catch up with Biplab, as well as former college and alumni at Uppsala University, Sweden, Iulia Emilia Brumboiu (now at Nicolaus Copernicus University, Poland) and Baotian Wang (now at Institute of High Energy Physics, CAS).
