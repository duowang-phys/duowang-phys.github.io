From <u>October 14</u> to <u>October 17</u>, 2025, two group members attended **the 3rd World Materials Conference (3rd WMC), 9th World Materials Summit, and IUMRS-ICA2025** held in **Guilin, China**, where they presented both an invited talk and a poster.
Duo Wang delivered an invited talk on multiferroic skyrmions discovered in the CrInX3 (X=Se, Te) system. PhD student Feng Chen presented a poster titled *Towards Universal Material Property Prediction with Deep Learning and Single-Descriptor Electronic Density*. They were awarded the **Certificate of Young Talents Invited Presentation** and **Certificate of Young Talents Invited Poster Presentation**, respectively.

In addition, we also had the opportunity to meet with Alumni from Sweden, including Prof. Dan Huang, Prof. Baotian Wang, Prof. Biswarup Pathak and Prof. Baisheng Sa.
